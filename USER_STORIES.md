# Warmpact — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Heat sales and account manager

- As an account manager, I want to generate a priced condition offer for a building from its underwriting result, so that I can sell a guarantee without waiting weeks for an engineering opinion.
- As an account manager, I want the offer to state the guarantee, exclusions, and the effect on the customer's annual expenditure in plain language, so that I am selling an overall solution rather than a tariff variant.
- As an account manager, I want to see when a customer's building is at risk of switching to an alternative heating method, so that I have a reason to be in the room before their next capital decision rather than after it.
- As an account manager, I want the exit and transfer terms visible in the offer, so that a customer worried about lock-in gets an answer instead of a reassurance.

### Service and underwriting analyst

- As an underwriting analyst, I want a building's thermal model, substation health, and envelope condition assessed before pricing, so that we do not guarantee a condition we cannot deliver at the price we quoted.
- As an underwriting analyst, I want a building we cannot underwrite to be declined or offered a remediation-first path, so that a bad risk becomes a project rather than a loss.
- As an underwriting analyst, I want to see how similar buildings have performed under their guarantees, so that pricing improves with portfolio experience rather than staying at the initial guess.

### Network operations and load planner

- As a load planner, I want a building-level heat demand forecast that accounts for weather and occupancy, so that supply temperature and production dispatch are planned against real demand rather than a design curve.
- As a load planner, I want to lower supply temperature where the connected buildings allow it and see which connections constrain me, so that network heat loss falls without breaching a guarantee.
- As a load planner, I want the connections with the worst return temperatures ranked by their cost to the network, so that I fix the ones that matter instead of the ones that complain.
- As a load planner, I want optimisation to be hard-blocked at the health and safety floors, so that an efficiency target cannot produce a legionella risk or a cold dwelling.

### Substation technician and energy advisor

- As a technician, I want a substation fault finding to arrive as a work order with the suspected cause and the evidence, so that I diagnose in the field rather than rediscover from scratch.
- As an energy advisor, I want to issue a remediation or retrofit proposal with its effect on the customer's long-run annual expenditure, so that I am the partner in their energy project instead of being absent from it.
- As an energy advisor, I want to compare a property against an anonymised cohort of comparable properties, so that my recommendation carries evidence the owner can act on.

### Billing, revenue assurance, and governance

- As a billing analyst, I want flat-rate invoices generated with automatic service credits where guarantees were breached, so that settlement is a calculation rather than a negotiation.
- As a revenue assurance analyst, I want gross margin per connection reported independently of delivered megawatt-hours, so that I can show the board whether decoupling is actually happening.
- As a tariff officer, I want to demonstrate that comparable customers are priced on comparable terms and that the condition product coexists with the published heat tariff, so that the product survives regulatory scrutiny.
- As a governance administrator, I want every guarantee change, price revision, and safety-floor override attempt logged with actor and rationale, so that an audit can reconstruct how a contract reached its current terms.
